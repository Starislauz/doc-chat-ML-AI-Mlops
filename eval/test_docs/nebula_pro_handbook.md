# Nebula Pro Smart Thermostat - Product Handbook

Version 3.2 - Published March 2026
Model: NB-PRO-3

## 1. Product Overview

The Nebula Pro Smart Thermostat is a wall-mounted climate controller designed
for residential heating systems. It combines a rotary dial with a 4-inch
touchscreen and learns the household's routine over a two-week calibration
period. The unit is manufactured by Halcyon Home Systems and sold through
authorised retailers and the Halcyon online store.

## 2. Physical Specifications

The Nebula Pro measures 84 mm wide, 84 mm tall and 22 mm deep, and weighs 210
grams without the mounting plate. The display is a 4-inch IPS panel with a
resolution of 480 by 480 pixels. The housing is flame-retardant polycarbonate
with a brushed aluminium front ring. The wall plate requires a standard UK or
EU single-gang back box.

## 3. Electrical Requirements

The Nebula Pro is designed for low-voltage HVAC systems only. It must be
connected to 24 VAC power and requires a C-wire (common wire). It is NOT
compatible with high-voltage systems running 110 V or 240 V mains directly.
Installing the unit on a high-voltage circuit will permanently damage the
device and voids the warranty. A licensed electrician should perform the
installation if a C-wire is not present at the thermostat location.

## 4. Connectivity

The Nebula Pro supports Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz (802.11 b/g/n), Bluetooth Low Energy 5.1
for proximity sensing, and Matter 1.2 for smart home integration. It works
with Google Home and Amazon Alexa ecosystems. Matter commissioning requires
the Nebula Home app version 4.0 or later. The device does not use the 5 GHz
Wi-Fi band; routers with band steering should create a separate 2.4 GHz SSID
during setup.

## 5. Battery

A CR2032 coin cell provides backup power for the clock and scheduled programs
during mains outages. Under normal operation the battery lasts up to 18
months. The dashboard in the Nebula Home app shows the remaining battery
level, and a low-battery warning appears on the thermostat screen when the
level drops below 10 percent.

## 6. Warranty

Every Nebula Pro carries a 3-year limited warranty from the date of purchase.
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal
residential use. To activate the full coverage, register the device in the
Nebula Home app within 30 days of purchase. Damage caused by incorrect
wiring, water ingress, or third-party accessories is excluded.

## 7. Pricing and Bundles

The Nebula Pro retails at $249 as a standalone unit. A bundle with two remote
temperature sensors retails at $329. Additional remote sensors are sold
separately at $45 each. A wall trim kit for larger back boxes costs $19.

## 8. Installation

Installation takes approximately 30 minutes. The mounting procedure is:
switch off the heating circuit, label the existing wires, attach the wall
plate, connect the wires to the labelled terminals, and clip the display unit
onto the plate. The thermostat automatically detects the connected system
type during first power-on.

## 9. Scheduling

The Nebula Pro supports up to eight temperature schedules per day, each with
its own target temperature. Schedules can be copied between weekdays and
weekends in the app. A holiday mode holds a fixed temperature for a chosen
number of days.

## 10. Energy Saving

Halcyon's internal field study of 2,100 households found that homes using the
Nebula Pro adaptive scheduling saved up to 14% on heating bills over a full
year. Savings depend on insulation, climate, and how closely the household
follows the suggested schedules.

## 11. Sensors and Accuracy

The built-in temperature sensor is accurate to plus or minus 0.3 degrees
Celsius, and the humidity sensor to plus or minus 2 percent. Two remote
sensor jacks are available for rooms with uneven heating. The thermostat
averages the readings of all connected sensors when more than one is
detected.

## 12. Factory Reset

To reset the Nebula Pro to factory settings, hold the rotary dial for 15
seconds until the LED flashes amber three times. Releasing the dial after
the amber pattern begins erases all schedules, Wi-Fi credentials, and the
calibration history. A factory reset does not deregister the device from the
Nebula Home app; use the "remove device" option for that.

## 13. Troubleshooting LED Codes

The status LED reports problems using blink patterns:

- LED flashes red twice: the thermostat has lost its Wi-Fi connection.
- LED flashes red five times: an internal sensor fault has been detected.
  Contact support and quote the error code shown in the app.
- LED flashes amber three times: a factory reset is in progress.

## 14. Companion App

The Nebula Home app is required for Wi-Fi setup and remote control. It is
available for iOS 16 and later and Android 12 and later. The app stores
temperature history for 12 months and supports geofencing, so the heating
drops to the away temperature when the last household member leaves.

## 15. Regulatory and Environment

The device is certified for CE and UKCA markets. Operating range is minus 20
to plus 50 degrees Celsius at up to 90 percent relative humidity,
non-condensing. The packaging is 100 percent recycled cardboard and the
device contains no mercury.
