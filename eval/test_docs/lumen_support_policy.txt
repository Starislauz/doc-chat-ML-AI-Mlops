LUMEN SUPPORT POLICY
Effective date: 1 January 2026
Version 4.1

1. SUPPORT HOURS
Our support desk operates Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 20:00 Central
European Time (CET). Outside these hours, coverage is limited to emergency
incidents only. Weekend staffing is reserved for incidents classified as
critical, defined as a complete outage of the workspace or data loss.

2. RESPONSE TIMES
Critical incidents receive a first response within 2 hours, around the
clock. Standard incidents receive a first response within 1 business day.
Low-priority questions receive a first response within 3 business days.
These targets are measured from the moment a ticket is created in the
support portal.

3. CONTACT CHANNELS
Support is available by email at support@lumen.example.com, through the
in-app chat widget, and by phone at +31 20 555 0134. Live chat is staffed
from 09:00 to 17:00 CET on weekdays. Email is monitored continuously during
support hours.

4. REFUNDS
Customers are entitled to a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no
questions asked. Refunds are processed to the original payment method
within 5 business days of the request. After the first 30 days, unused
credit on annual plans can be converted to account credit.

5. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Lumen guarantees 99.5% monthly uptime, measured across all production
regions. If uptime falls below this figure in a calendar month, affected
customers receive account credit equal to 10% of their monthly fee for each
full hour of downtime beyond the allowance.

6. PRIORITY SUPPORT
Priority support is available for $15 per month per workspace. Priority
customers receive a guaranteed first response within 30 minutes for
critical incidents and a dedicated support engineer familiar with their
workspace configuration.

7. DATA RETENTION
After cancellation, workspace data is retained for 90 days, during which
customers can export their content. After 90 days the data is permanently
deleted from all production systems and backups.

8. SECURITY
All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256.
Access logs are kept for 12 months. Customers may enable two-factor
authentication for all workspace members at no additional cost.

9. SERVICE REGIONS
Lumen production data is hosted in the European Union, with a secondary
region in Canada for disaster recovery. Customers can request a data
processing agreement from the support desk at any time.
